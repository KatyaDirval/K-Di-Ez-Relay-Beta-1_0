## What Relay does

Katya's Ez Relay is a lightweight Second Life companion focused on messaging, friends and contacts, group features, local chat, notecards, inventory viewing, World Map navigation, landmarks and convenient travel.

Beta 1.1 also adds saved avatar-license shortcuts, improved resident profiles, separate conference handling and real inventory-item insertion in notecards.

It is not a full graphical viewer, does not provide voice, and some tasks still require a conventional Second Life viewer.

## Windows standalone edition

Beta 1.1 introduces a standalone Windows x64 application using Microsoft Edge WebView2.

The standalone edition displays the existing Relay interface in its own desktop window while continuing to use the local Go backend and Second Life connection engine.

**System requirements:**

- Windows x64.
- Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen Runtime.
- An internet connection to access Second Life.
- A valid Second Life account.
- Optional K-Di registration for registered editions and paid features.

WebView2 Evergreen Runtime is maintained separately by Microsoft. It is not silently installed by the Relay installer. If it is unavailable, setup can direct you to Microsoft's official download page.

The standalone edition installs separately from the earlier browser-based edition. Do not run both editions simultaneously.

Locally saved conversations, licenses, contacts and other supported database records are not intentionally erased by installing or uninstalling the standalone application.

Some browser-profile information may require a separate transfer. Existing browser-only images, interface preferences or other locally stored browser records may not automatically appear in WebView2.

Where supported, selected browser-profile data can be transferred using the application's encrypted export/import process. Back up your existing records before moving between editions.

This standalone edition does not replace a conventional 3D Second Life viewer. Certain Second Life links, tasks and features may still require a separately installed viewer.

## Free editions and paid features

Forever Free requires no K-Di account or license validation. Optional K-Di Central news checks are recommended; a firewall may block K-Di Central without disabling normal Forever Free Grid features, but Second Life connectivity to Linden Lab's servers is required.

Registered Free is L$0 and requires separate account registration.

Contact and conversation limits apply:

- **Forever Free:** Up to 10 local contacts and 5 open conversations.
- **Registered Free:** Up to 25 contacts and 20 open conversations.

These limits do not restrict your actual Grid friends or memberships. Basic message-spam protection and saved chat history are available to everyone.

Unrestricted Chat is a separate permanent Premium Unlock that removes Relay's local contact, conversation and group limits and is required before buying a Pro term.

Other optional permanent Premium Unlocks are sold separately and may have prerequisites: Custom Themes, Mini Radar, Teleport to Avatar (requires Mini Radar), Your Image, Stop All Animations and the currently available Fatpack 1 bundle.

Check the purchase screen for availability and exact included functionality. Previously purchased permanent unlocks remain owned when Pro expires.

Pro is a time-limited expansion purchased separately after Unrestricted Chat. Pro lets you customize idle logout settings or disable automatic idle logout while the license is active.

It adds selected advanced messaging, notecard, group and business tools, subject to Grid permissions.

Optional per-group Auto-Messages and a separate, individually enabled Busy Auto-Reply for Group Chats and live conferences are supported; automatic messages are rate-limited and require the relevant live Grid session.

Pro includes saved-group recipient selection for landmarks and notecards: choose one person from the group, not a broadcast. Standard direct sending remains available under its normal feature rules.

Existing standalone landmark-group and historical Fatpack 1 rights remain valid for landmark group selection only; they do not grant notecard group selection. New standalone landmark-group sales are closed.

Available terms and prices appear at checkout and may differ between purchases made through Relay and those made inworld.

An expired term returns the account to Free without removing saved local content or permanent Premium Unlocks.

## Premium Unlock lifetime and possible free availability

Permanent Premium Unlocks are valid for the operational lifetime of Katya's Ez Relay System.

Purchased unlocks remain attached to the purchasing avatar while the application operates and supports the relevant features.

If Katya Dirval decides to stop updating Katya's Ez Relay System, she may choose to make some or all Premium Unlocks available free of charge. This is an option, not a guarantee.

Any such change will not revoke Premium Unlocks that were already purchased. Continued use may depend on Second Life service availability and compatibility.

**Grid Map, region search, map navigation, destination teleport and SLURL copying are included free in every edition, including Forever Free.**

They do not require a Premium Unlock or Pro. Grid access and landing rules still apply.

Movement Controls, Land Options, Estate Options and some inventory-management features are Coming Soon and unavailable to purchase.

Saved Chat History is included in every edition.

## Beta avatar and login limits

During the beta release, each user may use Katya's Ez Relay System with up to three avatars.

Each avatar must use its own valid Relay license where a registered license is required.

Only one avatar may be logged in to Relay at a time. We may increase the simultaneous-login limit to two or three avatars in a future update.

Until a change is announced, the one-avatar login limit continues to apply. Existing edition limits and license requirements still apply.

Saved avatar-license shortcuts can simplify account selection, but they do not store your Second Life password.

Removing a saved shortcut does not revoke the associated purchased license. Some saved-license shortcuts may require manually re-entering a valid license key during this Beta release.

## Inactivity logout

Forever Free disconnects after 60 minutes of inactivity, and Registered Free after 120 minutes.

Pro lets you customize your inactivity timeout or disable automatic idle logout completely through Settings.

Venue-specific rules may still apply at Katya's Chatroom and Free Parking.

If Relay disconnects, you can reconnect without losing saved local chats and drafts.

## Donations and payments

Forever Free can use a locally configured standalone inworld Pay tip jar, through Relay or an ordinary viewer.

It does not communicate with K-Di Central or enter its tipper rankings.

Relay retains only a local unresolved-attempt safety marker until the payer acknowledges checking Grid transaction history.

Registered editions can use separate Central-backed donation jars.

Public supporter names for those tracked jars appear only when the tipper selects Show Name. The payment recipient and Grid can identify every payer, even if Anonymous is selected.

Donations never grant Premium Unlocks or Pro features.

Verify amount and recipient before paying; ambiguous payments must be reconciled rather than repeated.

## Limitations and compatibility

Some Grid features depend on the connected avatar's permissions, the Grid's restrictions and network conditions.

For example, teleporting near another avatar uses a recent current-region position and can be prevented by landing rules.

Inventory and notecard permissions restrict what can be opened, embedded, copied or sent; an inventory offer may need recipient acceptance.

Embedded inventory items are existing Second Life items, not files uploaded from the computer.

Imported business CSVs are locally supplied records and are not verified account statements.

External parcel music URLs may be missing or unsafe. Check the full URL before connecting: stream providers may see your IP address and connection details, and a maliciously configured URL could link to other content.

Ez Relay warns before first playback and offers a Do not warn me again option.

Offline IM email forwarding is not yet available.

## Privacy, support and installation

Please read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) before installing.

K-Di Services never asks for your Second Life login credentials, and you should never reuse your Second Life password for a K-Di account.

Chats and other personal records are stored on your computer, and the complete local database is not encrypted.

K-Di Central handles account, license and purchase records for registered users, not your private conversations.

The Grid and sites you open follow their own policies.

Back up your local data before changing computers or uninstalling; removing application files alone does not erase saved records.
