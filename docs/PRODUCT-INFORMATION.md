## What Relay does

Katya's Ez Relay is a lightweight Grid companion focused on messaging, friends and contacts, group features, local chat, notecards, inventory viewing, landmarks and convenient travel. It is not a full graphical viewer, does not provide voice, and some tasks still require a conventional Grid viewer.

## Free editions and paid features

Forever Free requires no K-Di account and uses local licensing-free access. Registered Free is L$0 and requires account registration. Contact and conversation limits apply: Forever Free supports up to 10 local contacts and 5 open conversations; Registered Free supports up to 25 contacts and 20 open conversations. These limits do not restrict your actual Grid friends or memberships. Basic message-spam protection and saved chat history are available to everyone.

Unrestricted Chat is a separate permanent Premium Unlock that removes Relay's local contact, conversation and group limits and is required before buying a Pro term. Other optional permanent Premium Unlocks are sold separately and may have prerequisites: Custom Themes, Mini Radar, Teleport to Avatar (requires Mini Radar), Your Image, Stop All Animations and the currently available Fatpack 1 bundle. Check the purchase screen for availability and exact included functionality. Previously purchased permanent unlocks remain owned when Pro expires.

Pro is a time-limited expansion purchased separately after Unrestricted Chat. Pro lets you customize idle logout settings or disable automatic idle logout while the license is active. It adds selected advanced messaging, notecard, group and business tools, subject to Grid permissions. Pro includes saved-group recipient selection for landmarks and notecards: choose one person from the group, not a broadcast. Standard direct sending remains available under its normal feature rules.

Existing standalone landmark-group and historical Fatpack 1 rights remain valid for landmark group selection only; they do not grant notecard group selection. New standalone landmark-group sales are closed.

Available terms and prices appear at checkout and may differ between purchases made through Relay and those made inworld. An expired term returns the account to Free without removing saved local content or permanent Premium Unlocks.

## Premium Unlock lifetime and possible free availability

Permanent Premium Unlocks are valid for the operational lifetime of Katya's Ez Relay System. Purchased unlocks remain attached to the purchasing avatar while the application operates and supports the relevant features. If Katya Dirval decides to stop updating Katya's Ez Relay System, she may choose to make some or all Premium Unlocks available free of charge. This is an option, not a guarantee. Any such change will not revoke Premium Unlocks that were already purchased. Continued use may depend on Second Life service availability and compatibility.

Grid Map, Movement Controls, Land Options, Estate Options and some inventory-management features are Coming Soon and unavailable to purchase. Saved Chat History is included in every edition.

## Beta avatar and login limits

During the beta release, each user may use Katya's Ez Relay System with up to three avatars. Each avatar must use its own valid Relay license where a registered license is required. Only one avatar may be logged in to Relay at a time. We may increase the simultaneous-login limit to two or three avatars in a future update. Until a change is announced, the one-avatar login limit continues to apply. Existing edition limits and license requirements still apply.

## Inactivity logout

Forever Free disconnects after 60 minutes of inactivity, and Registered Free after 120 minutes. Pro lets you customize your inactivity timeout or disable automatic idle logout completely through Settings. Venue-specific rules may still apply at Katya’s Chatroom and Free Parking. If Relay disconnects, you can reconnect without losing saved local chats and drafts.

## Donations and payments

Forever Free can use a locally configured standalone inworld Pay tip jar, through Relay or an ordinary viewer. It does not communicate with K-Di Central or enter its tipper rankings. Relay retains only a local unresolved-attempt safety marker until the payer acknowledges checking Grid transaction history.

Registered editions can use separate Central-backed donation jars. Public supporter names for those tracked jars appear only when the tipper selects Show Name. The payment recipient and Grid can identify every payer, even if Anonymous is selected. Donations never grant Premium Unlocks or Pro features. Verify amount and recipient before paying; ambiguous payments must be reconciled rather than repeated.

## Payments and compatibility

Purchases must be made only through the official purchase flow or authorized K-Di Designs Shopping Terminals. Check the paying avatar, product, recipient, amount, duration and sales availability before confirming. Do not pay again while a purchase is awaiting confirmation. Your existing account key is retained after a successful entitlement refresh. Account registration and the account website do not receive Linden Dollar payments.

## Limitations and compatibility

Some Grid features depend on the connected avatar's permissions, the Grid's restrictions and network conditions. For example, teleporting near another avatar uses a recent current-region position and can be prevented by landing rules. Inventory and notecard permissions may restrict what can be opened, copied or sent. Imported business CSVs are locally supplied records and are not a verified account statement. Offline IM email forwarding is not yet available.

## Privacy, support and installation

Please read the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) before installing. Chats and other personal records are stored on your computer, and the complete local database is not encrypted. K-Di Central handles account, license and purchase records for registered users, not your private conversations. The Grid and sites you open follow their own policies. Back up your local data before changing computers or uninstalling; removing application files alone does not erase saved records.
