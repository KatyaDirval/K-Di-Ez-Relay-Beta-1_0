# Katya's Ez Relay privacy policy

Updated 2026-10-29 for release 1.0 (Beta). This policy explains what Relay stores on your computer, what K-Di Central handles for registered accounts, and what the Second Life Grid or third-party sites may receive.

For support, access, correction or deletion requests, contact Katya Dirval privately inworld. Do not send passwords or private conversations in support messages.

## Local use and Grid connections

Forever Free works without creating a K-Di account or checking a K-Di license. Its separate standalone inworld tip jar does not contact K-Di Central; normal Grid payment records are handled by Second Life. Central-backed donation services are available only to registered editions.

Every edition connects to the Grid you choose when you sign in. Grid credentials are submitted to that Grid using the local sign-in component; K-Di account services do not receive your Grid password, and Relay does not offer to save it. Grid messages and inventory operations pass through Grid services, which have their own terms and privacy practices. Relay does not provide end-to-end encryption for Grid messages.

Forever Free and Registered Free normally disconnect after inactivity; while Pro is active, you can customize or disable automatic idle logout in Settings. If you disable it, Relay may remain signed in until you disconnect or another applicable Grid or venue rule ends the session. Protect your device when leaving it unattended.

## Information saved on your computer

Relay saves conversations, imported chat logs, contacts and groups, inventory and notecard information, account associations, local license information, preferences and optional business transaction imports on your own device. Saved records generally remain after Sign Out or Quit Relay and can remain in local backups after uninstalling. The database as a whole is not encrypted. Protect access to your Windows account, computer and backup copies. Imported conversations and transaction records are not uploaded to K-Di Central as part of licensing.

Optional Your Image pictures are stored locally in the browser profile and are not uploaded to Central or the Grid by that feature. Removing the picture deletes the current browser copy, not necessarily older backups. Exported chat or transaction files can contain private data; protect them and get permission before sharing other people's messages.

## Registered accounts, licensing and purchases

If you choose Registered Free or buy a product, K-Di Central processes the avatar/account identifier, account sign-in information, license key and session/installation identifiers necessary to validate access. It also keeps account security records, entitlement and purchase details, product selection, payment amounts, transaction/receipt status, relevant timestamps and administrative audit records.

Separate account passwords are required; do not reuse your Grid password. Payment activity also creates records with the Grid operator. K-Di Central does not receive your chat contents, imported financial statements or contact lists through these license checks.

## Voluntary donations and visibility

Voluntary donations are paid to an owner-operated inworld tip jar. The standalone Forever Free tip jar is not linked to or tracked by K-Di Central; it keeps no local donation tally and does not contribute to cloud rankings. Relay retains only a local unresolved-attempt safety marker until you acknowledge checking Grid transaction history; it is not a donation ledger.

Registered editions can use separate Central-backed donation jars and statistics. Central stores the payer UUID, selected visibility, named-donation avatar name, amount, payment time, jar and merchant receipt identifiers for history and reconciliation. Anonymous means hidden from public names and rankings; the payment recipient and Grid still identify the payer.

Only named contributions enter public supporter totals. A later named donation never unmasks earlier anonymous contributions. Public totals include anonymous payments, and the last and highest individual donation records show Anonymous when selected.

The recipient keeps private payment history, protected backups may retain it, and no general automatic deletion deadline is currently implemented. Contact Katya Dirval privately for access or correction. Donations do not grant Premium features or licenses.

## Other features and third parties

Resident searches, friends, group membership information, status, current-region proximity, teleports, inventory and landmark operations obtain data from the connected Grid. A landmark or notecard you intentionally send reveals its contents or location to the recipient. Nearby local chat is visible to others in range.

Mini Radar is designed for current-region use and does not send tracked positions to K-Di Central.

Image and profile previews may be fetched from the Grid and, when an image is unavailable on the main Grid, from an official Grid web service. Those providers receive normal connection information and the requested image identifier. Links you deliberately open contact their respective sites. Offline email integration is under construction and unavailable in this release.

## Purpose, rights and contact

Account and licensing information is used to supply requested registration and paid services, prevent fraudulent payment claims and secure those services. Donation receipt details are processed to reconcile inworld payments and display only the visibility choices described above.

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or objection, and may contact your local data-protection authority. Some receipt and security records may need to be retained for legitimate reconciliation or legal obligations. Contact Katya Dirval privately inworld. The operator will explain any applicable retention restrictions when responding. Transfers to the Grid and websites you choose are subject to those services' separate policies.

## Retention, choices and removal

Local records are retained until you remove them; uninstalling the application does not automatically erase its saved data. K-Di Central currently retains accounts, licenses, purchase and audit records until the operator removes them, subject to operational, payment or legal requirements; there is no general automatic deletion deadline currently. Backup copies may persist longer. Contact Katya Dirval privately to request access, correction or deletion and to establish control of the relevant account without disclosing a Grid password.
